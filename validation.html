Flown against the book

Every aircraft in Cayley is built from published geometry and flown, headless, against its own handbook. This page is the result of that run, misses included.

Handbook figures and tolerances live in each aircraft's definition file under js/aircraft/. Section properties, parasite drag and propeller pitch were tuned within physical ranges to meet them, so the handbook rows show that each model performs like its aircraft. The wind-tunnel rows (lift-curve slope, span efficiency, loading, ground effect) and the handling rows are not tuned for: they are what the vortex model produces.
